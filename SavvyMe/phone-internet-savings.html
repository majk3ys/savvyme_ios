Ever looked at your phone or internet bill and thought, How did it get this high? You're not alone! The good news? With a few smart moves, you can slash your bills without sacrificing speed, reliability, or binge-watching sessions. Here's how to maximise savings on your phone and internet plans.

1. Bring Your Own Phone – Avoid Expensive Bundles 📱

Phone + plan bundles might seem convenient, but they often cost more in the long run. Buying your phone outright gives you the freedom to choose a cheaper plan – see 2. Choose Prepaid or SIM-Only Plans. Retailers like JB Hi-Fi, Big W, and Apple often sell phones at lower prices than telcos, especially during sales and promotions—so always compare before buying!

Here's an example:

Option Inclusions Cost Over 36 months
Telstra iPhone 16 + plan iPhone 16 + 50GB data, 5G, unlimited calls & texts (Basic Plan) $1,398 (phone only) + $65/month = $3,738
Buy iPhone 16 outright + FelixMobile iPhone 16 + 50GB data, 5G, unlimited calls & texts (FelixMobile Prepaid) $1,317 (JB Hi-Fi) + $30/month = $2,397

Total savings: $1,341 over 3 years.

2. 💳 Choose Prepaid or SIM-Only Plans

Once you own your phone, you can switch to a budget-friendly plan. Prepaid and SIM-only deals often cost 50-70% less than traditional postpaid plans. Smaller providers like Amaysim, Kogan, Boost, and FelixMobile actually use the same networks as the big telcos (e.g. Boost uses Telstra, Amaysim uses Optus, Kogan uses Vodafone), so you're not sacrificing coverage!

Example:

Plan type Data allowance Monthly cost Annual cost
Big telco postpaid 30GB ~$50 $600
Big telco prepaid 30GB ~$40 $480
Alternative provider prepaid 30GB ~$25 $300

3. Periodically Review & Compare Plans 🔄

Many people stick with the same plan for years, overpaying without realising. Phone and internet providers regularly adjust pricing and offer new deals—so shopping around every 12 months can save you hundreds.

📌 Check comparison sites to find the best deal:

  • WhistleOut
  • Finder
  • Canstar Blue

💡 Tip: Switching plans is easier than ever with eSIMs, which allow you to activate a new provider instantly—no need to wait for a SIM card in the mail!

4. Don't Skimp on The Plans…If You Are a Serious Data User 📶

Going over your data limit? Extra charges add up fast! Instead of paying overage fees, upgrade to a plan that actually meets your needs.

Example:

Plan Data limit Monthly cost Extra data used (5GB) Total monthly cost (including extras)
Budget plan 10GB $15 $25 ($5 per GB) $40
Mid-tier plan 40GB $25 $0 (no extra fees) $25

Total savings: $15+ per month saved

💡 Tip: Check your usage in your provider's app and adjust your plan accordingly

5. 🏠 Consider 4G/5G Instead of NBN

Not a hardcore gamer? A 4G/5G home internet plan might be a cheaper and more flexible alternative to NBN.

Plan type Monthly cost
NBN 50/100 $80+
4G/5G home internet $55-$70

Total savings: $25+ per month saved

💡 Tip: Many providers offer a free trial for 4G/5G home internet—test it before committing!

6. 📜 Read the Fine Print – Avoid Hidden Fees

Before locking into a plan, check for hidden costs like:

  • ✔️ Exit fees (early contract termination penalties)
  • ✔️ Modem charges (some "free" modems require repayment if you leave early)
  • ✔️ Penalty fees (missed payments can lead to unexpected charges)

💡 Tip: Always read the critical information summary before signing up

Final Thoughts 💰

With these smart strategies, you could save as much as $500 per year on phone and internet bills. The key? Stay flexible, compare deals regularly, and avoid unnecessary charges.
