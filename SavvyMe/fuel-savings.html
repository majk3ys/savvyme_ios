1. Shop Around with PetrolSpy

Instead of heading to the nearest petrol station and hoping for the best, use PetrolSpy to compare fuel prices in your area. This handy tool works across Australia and lets you filter by fuel type (U91, U95, Diesel, etc.).

Why it works:

  • Prices can vary significantly between petrol stations, especially when some outlets are slower to hike prices.
  • Helps you identify stations with excess supply offering lower rates.
  • Can save you 10-30c per litre depending on timing and location.

2. Redeem Grocery Vouchers

If you shop at Coles or Woolworths, you're probably already eligible for fuel discounts without even knowing it.

  • Coles & Shell: Spend $30 at Coles and get 4c per litre off at Shell by scanning your docket or linking your Flybuys account. More details here.
  • Woolworths & Ampol/Caltex: Spend $30 at Woolworths and get 4c per litre off at Ampol or Caltex when you scan your receipt or Everyday Rewards card. Learn more here.

How much can you save?

A typical 50L tank means a saving of $2 per fill-up—not bad for a freebie!

3. Lock in Low Prices with 7-Eleven's Fuel Lock

7-Eleven's Fuel Lock feature allows you to lock in a low price for up to seven days. If you see a great deal today but don't need to refuel yet, lock it in via the 7-Eleven app and redeem it later at any 7-Eleven in Australia. Learn more here.

Maximum savings:

  • Prices can rise by 10-25c per litre in a week, so locking in a lower price can save up to $12.50 on a 50L tank.
  • Note: The Fuel Lock feature caps savings at 25c per litre.

4. Leverage Automobile Club Discounts

Are you a member of an automobile club? Many clubs offer fuel discounts that can be combined with grocery rewards for bigger savings. Examples:

  • RACV (VIC): Save 5c per litre at EG Ampol. Stack it with Woolworths' 4c per litre discount for a total of 9c per litre off. More info here.
  • NRMA (NSW): Save 5c per litre at Ampol Foodary. Combine it with Woolworths' discount for 9c off per litre. Check details here.
  • RACQ (QLD): Save 4c per litre every day at participating Caltex and Puma Energy stations across QLD by scanning your RACQ card. Find locations here.

Example savings:

  • 50L fill-up with combined discounts = $4.50 saved (RACV/NRMA + Woolworths).
  • 70L fill-up (larger SUVs, diesel vehicles) = $6.30 saved.

5. Use Credit Card/Bank Cashback Offers

Certain credit card rewards programs and bank offers provide limited-time fuel discounts:

  • American Express sometimes offers $10 off Ampol fuel purchases.
  • CommBank Yello frequently provides cashback deals at Ampol.
  • Other major banks periodically offer cashback on fuel—check your online banking app for promotions.

How does cashback work?

  • After activating an offer in your credit card or bank account, the discount is usually credited back to your statement within a few days.
  • No need for a code or voucher—just pay with the linked card to receive the benefit.
  • Pro tip: Regularly check your bank or credit card app for fuel offers, as they change frequently.

BONUS. Earn Rewards Points with BP & Velocity

Fuel up and earn points you can redeem for flights, shopping, or more fuel:

  • BP Rewards: Earn Qantas Frequent Flyer points on every fill-up.
  • Velocity & 7-Eleven: Link your Velocity account to earn points on every dollar spent on fuel.
